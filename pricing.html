Questions

What happens when the free trial ends?

Your campaigns pause automatically. Upgrade any time to reactivate them with no setup needed.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes — upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your account at any time. Changes apply on your next billing date.

Is there a limit on ad spend?

No — your plan covers VAST's usage, not your ad budget. You control ad spend directly with each platform.
